## About
 
I help people and organizations make complex systems easier to understand, use, and trust — for human audiences and, increasingly, for the AI systems that read, summarize, and cite content on their behalf. My work sits at the intersection of UX, Information Architecture, AI visibility, and learning design.
 
---
 
## Creator of UCBO
 
I developed **UCBO (User-Centric Business Ontology)**, a proprietary framework that reveals the gap between how organizations understand themselves, how users actually experience them, and how AI systems represent them.
 
Instead of treating UX, Information Architecture, and AI visibility as separate disciplines, UCBO combines them into a single diagnostic process that produces a prioritized, actionable roadmap. It's the foundation of every audit and consulting engagement I run.
